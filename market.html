How it's computed: for removed postings that are not first-run postings, removed_date − first_seen in days. The heading reports the median; the chart shows how many postings closed after each number of days.

…

How it's computed: among postings that are not first-run postings and were first seen at least 7 days before the last run, the share with a removed_date where removed_date − first_seen ≤ 7 days.

06 · Top locations

…

How it's computed: open postings counted by their location text as the board gives it (Workday boards list several-city postings as "N Locations"); the 15 most common are shown.

Postings with first_run = true were already open when the tool started watching their board, so their start date is unknown; findings 1, 4 and 5 leave them out.
